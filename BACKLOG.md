# Backlog (bookmarked, not in scope yet)

Ideas captured during planning. Nothing here is built until pulled into a milestone.

## Feel and clarity
- Juice pass: hit stop, screen shake, carve sound, damage numbers.
- Early digging should be fast and dramatic: hull layer cleared in ~30-40 s, tune with real numbers.
- Distinct color/brightness per layer, depth shadows so carving reads as depth.
- Per-layer progress HUD (hull 62%, frame 0%) plus room status.
- Bullet readability: bright enemy bullets against a darker boss.

## Gameplay
- Risk/reward lever: combo or bonus for flying close / chaining explosive consoles.
- Player dodge or bomb on a cooldown (screen clear or short invulnerability).
- Rooms fight back when first exposed (shield burst, drone).
- Enrage: patterns escalate once the boss is below a damage threshold.
- Sideways/arc carving tools to avoid the "straw shaft" problem: homing missiles, bombs, angled shots.
- Multiple approach paths to each room so boss design is a small maze.
- Boss motion variety: rotate, mirror, zip side to side (sway is first).
- Hull regrowth, per-boss flag only.
- Keep boss 1 simple; introduce one new mechanic per boss.

## Decisions already made
- Death resets the fight (boss damage resets too).
- Destroying the core (star) wins.
- Rooms contain consoles; destroying all consoles in a room disables that room's function.
- Pre-mission action report: boss layout, rooms, and what each room affects.
